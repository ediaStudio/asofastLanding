Frequently asked questions

What is AsoFast?

AsoFast is a self-hosted, open source tool for App Store and Google Play listing optimization. It generates metadata, translations, keywords, and screenshot templates from a local dashboard.

Is AsoFast really free?

Yes. AsoFast is released under the MIT license. There are no usage limits, subscriptions, or credit cards required.

Do I need to share my App Store credentials?

No. AsoFast runs locally on your machine. Your App Store Connect and Google Play Console credentials stay with you.

Which stores are supported?

AsoFast supports both Apple App Store and Google Play Store listings, including metadata fields, screenshots, and localization workflows.

Can I use my own AI API key?

Yes. Bring your own API key so you control costs, model choice, and data privacy. No inference runs through our servers.

How does the screenshot generator work?

Upload your raw screenshots and the generator frames them for iPhone, iPad, Android phones, and Android tablets in the sizes each store requires.
