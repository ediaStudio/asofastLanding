I remember the month I spent more on Meta ads than my app made in revenue. I was one bad campaign away from quitting, and I know a lot of you are in the same spot: you built something good, you shipped it, and then nothing happened. No downloads. No reviews. Just the quiet panic of watching your ad budget burn while TikTok and Meta keep asking for more.

Here is the thing nobody tells you when you start: paid acquisition is a subscription, not an investment. The moment you stop paying, the downloads stop. The only channel that keeps paying you back long after you stop working on it is organic traffic, and for apps, that means App Store Optimization.

So you go looking for ASO tools, and that is where the second shock hits. The good ones cost more than your hosting bill, your coffee budget and your therapy sessions combined.

## What the big ASO platforms really cost

Let me save you the sales calls. These are the prices I found by actually checking pricing pages in 2026:

- **AppTweak**: $79/month for the Essential plan, $299 for Grow, $549 for Grow Plus. No free plan.
- **Sensor Tower**: no public pricing at all. You book a call and get a quote. Third-party data puts entry at around $500/month, and the median contract is about $74,000 per year.
- **App Radar**: a usable free tier, then $69/month for the ASO plan.
- **ASODesk**: $29.99/month for Startup, $59.99 for Optimal. The budget option of the serious platforms.
- **Screenshots Pro**: $19/month for a screenshot generator.
- **Appfigures**: $7.99/month entry, but it is analytics first, ASO second.

Add that up and a "proper" ASO stack for one indie developer is $100 to $600 a month before you even touch your app. That is a car payment. For a tool you will open twice a week.

I am not saying these products are bad. AppTweak's keyword research is genuinely strong, and if you run ten apps with real revenue, it can pay for itself. But here is the uncomfortable truth: most of what these platforms sell you is rank tracking and market intelligence. If your app is still fighting for its first thousand downloads, you do not need to track your position on 5,000 keywords. You need better metadata, in more languages, published to the right stores. That part should not cost you $600 a month.

## The 7 tools worth knowing in 2026

**1. AppTweak ($79/month)**. The reference for keyword research and competitive analysis. Excellent data, clean reports. Overkill for a first app, fair for a serious studio.

**2. Sensor Tower (quote only)**. The enterprise standard. Unbeatable market intelligence, unreachable price for an indie. If you are getting pitched Sensor Tower as a solo dev, ask them why.

**3. App Radar (free, then $69/month)**. The best free tier of the big platforms. You can track a handful of keywords for one app without paying. Outgrows you quickly, but a fine place to learn.

**4. ASODesk ($29.99/month)**. The budget pick. Solid keyword tools and localization support. Feels less polished than AppTweak, costs a third as much.

**5. Screenshots Pro ($19/month)**. Niche and honest about it: it makes store screenshots. Good templates, fair price. You still have to write the metadata yourself.

**6. Appfigures ($7.99/month)**. Great for download and revenue numbers across stores. Less useful for actually improving your listing.

**7. AsoFast ($0, open source)**. This is the one I ended up building, so I will be direct about what it does and does not do. [AsoFast](https://github.com/ediaStudio/asofast) runs entirely on your machine, generates your App Store and Google Play metadata (title, subtitle, description, keywords) with AI, translates it into 80+ languages, produces screenshot templates, and publishes the listings straight to App Store Connect and Google Play. Your data never leaves your computer. There is no subscription, no account, no server that stores your credentials. It is MIT licensed, so you can read every line of code.

## Why most listings leave downloads on the table

One thing I see constantly: developers publish their app in English only, in one store, and then wonder why the numbers do not move. The App Store and Google Play are global. An app about football will get far more traction in markets where football is the national sport. A habit tracker will find paying users in wealthy markets and volume in emerging ones. Some languages bring downloads, others bring revenue, and you cannot know which until you are actually listed in them.

AsoFast handles that part in one pass: generate the base listing, translate it, review what it produced, publish. The whole loop takes minutes, not weeks, and it costs nothing.

## My honest advice

If you have a budget and a portfolio of apps, buy AppTweak and stop reading. If you are a solo developer trying to get your first real downloads without going bankrupt on ads, do not sign up for a $600 monthly stack you will barely use. Start free. Fix your metadata, localize, publish properly, and only pay for intelligence once you have something to optimize.

Your app deserves better than being invisible because you could not afford a dashboard.
